The 5% floor

Every brand on Turnpike carries a standing 5% manufacturer chargeback, so buyers learn there is always a deal here. It is the reason they come back without being asked.

That 5% is not our fee. It is promotional funding that goes to the retailer. We never take a percentage of it, or of anything else you sell.

The guarantee

  • Pro-rata, on new doors and new placements. Not on revenue — we don't control your velocity, so we don't promise it.
