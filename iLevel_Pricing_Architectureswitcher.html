Holding what you win

Every quarter, from data

A brand in three thousand doors loses more to quiet voids in a quarter than it gains from two new chains. We find them from order data across every DC — which doors stopped buying, which items fell out of the set, which stores never took the full line — and put a plan against each one.

Most brokers find a void when someone happens to walk that store. We find it in the data, on a schedule. Holding your existing points of distribution is the earliest and cheapest revenue available to you.
